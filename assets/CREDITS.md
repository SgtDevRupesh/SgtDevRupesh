# Image and font credits

Photos are U.S. government works in the public domain, cut out from the originals on Wikimedia Commons:

- `f16_*` — F-16 Fighting Falcon over Iraq, U.S. Air Force. <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:F-16_June_2008.jpg>
- `patriot_*` — MIM-104 Patriot launcher, U.S. Army. <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MIM-104_Patriot_surface-to-air_missile_system_launcher.jpg>
- `harm_*` — AGM-88 HARM on display, U.S. Air Force. <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:AGM-88_HARM_in_hangar.jpg>

Each photo is stored as a colour JPEG plus a greyscale luminance mask (`*_mask.jpg`); the SVG combines them for transparency.
